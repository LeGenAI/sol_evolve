\begin{abstract}
We present SolEvolve, a framework for automated algorithm discovery that integrates SAT-verified formal guarantees with closed-loop semantic feedback. Unlike prior LLM-evolutionary approaches relying on scalar fitness metrics, SolEvolve's Reflector component autonomously analyzes verification outputs, generates causal hypotheses about failure modes, and synthesizes targeted interventions without human involvement.

The framework successfully navigated complex combinatorial spaces to discover novel error-correcting codes with optimized weight distributions, and the first perfect partition of $\Lambda_7(1^4)$ below Mollard's boundary. An ablation study shows that semantic feedback improves SAT-GA repair success rate from 2\% to 10\% compared to fitness-only baselines. The Generator also derived a rank-one elimination algorithm for self-orthogonal embeddings; we provide evidence this represents genuine synthesis rather than retrieval by testing on a novel problem formulation absent from training data. These results demonstrate LLMs as effective components in formally-verified mathematical discovery pipelines.
\end{abstract}
