\bio{}
\textbf{Jon-Lark Kim} received his B.S. degree in mathematics from Pohang University of Science and Technology (POSTECH), finished the master’s course in mathematics from Seoul National University, and received his Ph.D. in mathematics from the University of Illinois at Chicago, in 1993, 1997, and 2002, respectively. He joined the University of Nebraska-Lincoln from 2002 to 2005 as a research assistant professor. He joined the University of Louisville between 2005 and 2012 as an assistant and association professor. Since 2012, he has been a professor at the Department of Mathematics at Sogang University, Seoul, Korea. His research interests include coding theory, cryptography, soft computing, and artificial intelligence.
\endbio
