\section*{Data availability}
The complete source code for the SolEvolve framework, including the Generator, Evolver, and Verifier modules, is available at \url{https://github.com/LeGenAI/sol_evolve.git}. The experimental logs, generated SAT instances, and discovery artifacts (generator matrices) are archived in the same repository to ensure reproducibility. The specific prompt templates used for the LLM Generator are also provided.
